# 🎓 EuroTrack

### Your 2027 European Master's Application Tracker

EuroTrack is a university admissions tracking website designed to help students organise their European Master's applications for the 2027 intake.

Explore universities, monitor application deadlines, track scholarship opportunities and manage your shortlist — all in one place.

---

## 🌍 Explore Your Study Destinations

EuroTrack currently covers four destinations and programme categories:

- 🇮🇹 **Italy** — Explore universities, admission windows and application deadlines.
- 🇩🇪 **Germany** — Track university applications and DAAD scholarship milestones.
- 🇸🇪 **Sweden** — Monitor university admissions and Swedish Institute scholarship dates.
- 🎓 **Erasmus Mundus** — Explore joint Master's programmes, scholarship opportunities and application deadlines.

## ✨ Key Features

- **University Directory** — Browse and explore shortlisted universities.
- **Application Tracking** — Monitor upcoming application windows and closing dates.
- **Deadline Timeline** — View important admission and scholarship milestones chronologically.
- **Watchlist** — Save universities and keep track of your preferred options.
- **Smart Filtering** — Filter universities by application status and search by university or city.
- **QS Rankings** — View available university ranking information.
- **Responsive Design** — Access the tracker on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- **Centralised Dashboard** — Keep important application information organised in one place.

## 📌 Important Note

EuroTrack is an independent student planning project. Application deadlines, admission requirements, scholarship criteria and university information may change.

Always verify important information through the official university or scholarship programme website before applying.

EuroTrack is an organisational tool and is not affiliated with any university, the DAAD, the Swedish Institute or the Erasmus Mundus programme.
