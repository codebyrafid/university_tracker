GrEnFIn

Greening Energy Market and Finance · Environmental Finance / Climate & Business
Priority 1
Application deadline7 January 2027 · 11:00 CET

A 2-year, 120-ECTS joint master focused on energy markets, sustainable finance and the green transition. Tracks: Renewable Technologies, Environmental Finance, and Climate & Business. 51 places (17 per track).

Eligibility watch: three-year degree/equivalent plus relevant curricular background in Mathematics, Physics, Economics, Statistics, IT or Engineering. A BBA Finance degree needs careful curriculum/ISCED assessment; not automatically confirmed.
Timeline, requirements & documents

Timeline

  • Applications opened: 14 October 2026, 11:00 CET
  • Deadline: 7 January 2027, 11:00 CET
  • Results: 26 February–1 March 2027
  • Acceptance/pre-enrolment: 1–8 March 2027
  • Matriculation: 8–15 March 2027
  • Programme starts: 6 September 2027

Admission requirements

  • Three-year university degree or equivalent, recognised as suitable.
  • Curricular requirements in Mathematics, Physics, Economics, Statistical Sciences, IT or Engineering; foreign degrees are assessed for suitability.
  • English at CEFR B2 or above, proven with a recognised certificate.
  • Academic merit and overall experience; GRE/GMAT optional.

Required documents

  • Valid identity document (passport if ID is not in English).
  • Completed ISCED Classification Template.
  • Bachelor diploma and transcript (foreign documents translated into Italian or English).
  • Recognised English certificate.
  • Overall experience using the Europass template.
  • GRE/GMAT certificate optional.

Important notices

  • Upload every mandatory document through Studenti Online; email/post submissions are not assessed.
  • Incomplete mandatory documents make the application ineligible for assessment.
  • Scholarship: full-time only; previous EMJM scholarship holders cannot receive another EMJM scholarship; cannot combine with another EU-funded scholarship for the same course.
  • Scholarship ranking is shared across all three tracks and subject to geographical-balance rules.

GLOBAL COOP

Economic Policies for New Global Cooperation and Alliances · Track 2: International Monetary & Financial System
Priority 2
Application deadline27 January 2027 · 13:00 Paris time

A two-year economics-focused joint master covering international trade, the international monetary and financial system, and the just socio-ecological transition.

Eligibility watch: Economics degree, or another degree with at least 24 ECTS (or equivalent) in Economics and 6 ECTS in Statistics/Econometrics. Track 1 has an additional four-year-degree/equivalent condition.
Timeline, requirements & documents

Timeline

  • Call opened: October 2026
  • Deadline: 27 January 2027, 13:00 Paris time
  • Interviews: March 2027
  • Results: end of March 2027
  • Programme begins: September 2027

Admission requirements

  • Bachelor/equivalent degree of 180 ECTS.
  • Economics degree, or at least 24 ECTS Economics plus 6 ECTS Statistics/Econometrics (or equivalent).
  • English: CEFR C1 or accepted equivalent; IELTS Academic 7.0 minimum.
  • Clear motivation and fit with the programme; track-specific conditions apply.

Required documents

  • Online application form.
  • Full CV (Europass recommended, not mandatory).
  • Degree certificate and complete academic transcript, with English translations where required.
  • English proficiency proof.
  • Statement of purpose, maximum 500 words.
  • Two recommendation letters from professors/teachers or job supervisors.
  • Valid passport copy.
  • Other funding details optional.

Important notices

  • Documents are uploaded through the online portal, not sent by email.
  • Start early: recommendation letters take time.
  • Selected applicants may need certified/legalised academic records for enrolment.
  • Check the chosen track and major/minor requirements carefully before applying.

EPOG-JM

Economic Policies for the Global Bifurcation · Macroeconomics, Finance & Socioeconomic Transformation
Priority 3
2-year programme deadline27 January 2027 · 13:00 Paris time

A two-year joint master in economics examining digital, socio-economic and ecological transitions through pluralist and interdisciplinary approaches.

Eligibility watch: Economics degree, or another degree with at least 24 ECTS in Economics and 6 ECTS in Statistics/Econometrics. Published selection criteria include a minimum GPA of 70% of the maximum scale.
Timeline, requirements & documents

Timeline

  • Call opened: October 2026
  • Deadline: 27 January 2027, 13:00 Paris time
  • Interviews: March 2027
  • Results: end of March 2027
  • Programme begins: September 2027

Admission requirements

  • Bachelor/equivalent degree of 180 ECTS.
  • Economics degree, or at least 24 ECTS Economics plus 6 ECTS Statistics/Econometrics (or equivalent).
  • English proficiency proof; check the programme's accepted-test list and any minor-specific conditions.
  • Selection considers academic background, academic records, motivation/recommendations and languages. Published GPA criterion: at least 70% of the maximum scale.

Required documents

  • Completed online application.
  • Full CV.
  • Degree certificate and academic transcript(s), with English translations where required.
  • English proficiency proof.
  • Statement of purpose, maximum 500 words.

Important notices

  • Academic eligibility is not guaranteed by meeting the general degree rule alone; course-credit requirements matter.

EGEI

Economics of Globalisation and European Integration
Priority 4
Scholarship application deadline28 February 2027

A two-year master in international economics, globalisation, European integration and international economic relations. Scholarship call offers 10 full scholarships for 2027–28.

Eligibility watch: 180 ECTS in Economics, Applied Economics or related disciplines, with a solid economics and quantitative-analysis foundation. Your transcript needs course-level review.
Timeline, requirements & documents

Timeline

  • Platform opened: 15 November 2026
  • Scholarship deadline: 28 February 2027
  • Applicants needing a visa: programme recommends applying by 30 March 2027 for non-scholarship consideration only.
  • Self-funded applications (where visa not needed): up to 30 May 2027, subject to eligibility.
  • Shortlisted interviews: likely last week of March 2027
  • Programme starts: early September 2027.

Admission requirements

  • 180 ECTS bachelor-level degree or higher in Economics, Applied Economics or related disciplines.
  • Solid economics background, including quantitative analysis (e.g. micro/macro, econometrics/statistics, international economics).
  • English proficiency certificate; consult the programme's accepted proof list.

Required documents

  • ID/passport and proof of residence.
  • Degree diploma(s) and official transcript of records.
  • Legalised copy of degree certificate(s) for the subsequent verification/enrolment stage.
  • Motivation letter.
  • Up-to-date Europass CV.
  • English proficiency proof.
  • Contact details for 1–3 referees (first referee must be academic).
  • Optional evidence of additional skills/qualifications.

Important notices

  • Scholarship applications by 28 February are automatically considered for the 2027–28 scholarship competition.
  • Scholarship package stated by the programme: fee waiver plus €1,400/month, with 10 full scholarships announced on the programme page.
  • Scholarship and self-funded deadlines differ; later submission does not qualify for scholarship consideration.
  • Original degree documents are checked on arrival at the first host institution.

M3EP

European Environmental Economics & Policy
Priority 5
Non-EU/EEA/Swiss application deadline15 January 2027 · 23:59 CET

A two-year environmental economics and policy joint master: first year at the University of Copenhagen, second-year specialisation at one of four partner universities; double degree.

Eligibility caution: international applicants need at least 60 ECTS in Economics and/or Political Science. Finance credits are not automatically equivalent; map your transcript.
Timeline, requirements & documents

Timeline

  • Non-EU/EEA/Swiss application window opened: 15 November 2026
  • Deadline for applicants from outside EU/EEA/Switzerland: 15 January 2027, 23:59 CET
  • EU/EEA/Swiss application window opens: 16 January 2027; deadline 1 March 2027, 23:59 CET
  • Non-EU results: late March to May 2027
  • Programme starts: September 2027 (check offer/handbook for exact arrival dates).

Admission requirements

  • Relevant bachelor-level degree and required subject-credit background.
  • At least 60 ECTS in Economics and/or Political Science for international bachelor qualifications, as stated in the programme admission criteria.
  • English proficiency and academic documentation according to University of Copenhagen requirements.

Required documents

  • Prepare the documents specified in the University of Copenhagen application portal and M3EP admission procedure, including degree certificate and transcript.
  • English-language proof and motivation letter as required by the official application instructions.
  • Supporting documents must meet the official certification/translation rules.

Important notices

  • Non-EU deadline is earlier than the EU/EEA/Swiss deadline; Bangladesh applicants follow the non-EU schedule.
  • Scholarship holders must complete the required mobility across two countries different from their country of residence at application.
  • Do not assume BBA Finance courses count toward the 60-ECTS Economics/Political Science requirement without programme confirmation.

GLODEP

Global Development Policy
Priority 6
Scholarship application deadline31 January 2027

A two-year programme in global development policy, economic development and policy analysis, delivered by Palacký University Olomouc, University of Clermont Auvergne and University of Göttingen.

Eligibility caution: at least 60 ECTS in Economics and C1 English. Non-French/non-German applicants need GPA equivalent to at least 2.7/4.0; your 2.80 meets the stated numerical threshold only if formally considered equivalent.
Timeline, requirements & documents

Timeline

  • Scholarship/first call: 5 November 2026–31 January 2027
  • Self-funded second call: 1 February–15 March 2027
  • Admission results: second half of March (first call)
  • Scholarship interviews: first half of April
  • Scholarship decisions: second half of April
  • Programme starts: mid-September 2027.

Admission requirements

  • At least 60 ECTS (or equivalent) in Economics during the bachelor’s degree.
  • For applicants outside German/French grading systems, GPA equivalent to at least 2.7/4.0.
  • English at CEFR C1; accepted IELTS minimum is 7.0.
  • Strong interest in international development and development policy.

Required documents

  • Scan of original degree diploma.
  • Transcript of records or diploma supplement, including completed courses.
  • Accepted English proficiency proof.
  • Europass CV, maximum three A4 pages.
  • Motivation letter, maximum 500 words.
  • Valid international passport (programme states validity through at least September 2029).

Important notices

  • Only PDF attachments in English are accepted (diploma exception as specified); incomplete applications are not assessed.
  • Diploma certification/recognition is not required at application stage; it follows after admission.
  • Documents cannot be added or changed after submission; submit and pay before the deadline.
  • The 1 February–15 March second call is for self-funded applicants only, not scholarship applicants.
  • Selection may include an online interview for scholarship competition.
